Privacy Policy for DocScan PDF

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

🔒 Core Privacy Promise: DocScan PDF is a 100% offline document and ID card scanner. Your scanned images, documents, and generated PDF files never leave your device. We do not operate external servers, databases, or cloud sync services.

1. Introduction

Welcome to DocScan PDF ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your personal files. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DocScan PDF mobile application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because DocScan PDF operates as a local on-device utility:

  • No Personal Documents: We never upload, view, transmit, or store your scanned receipts, identity cards, photos, or generated PDF documents on any remote server.
  • No Account Required: The app does not require registration, login, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Zero Cloud Databases: All document data resides exclusively in your device's private local storage.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

To deliver scanning and file generation functionality, DocScan PDF requires the following standard Android permissions:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly to take photos of documents and ID cards in real time within the scanner viewfinder. The camera is active only while you are actively scanning.
  • Photo & Media Access: Used exclusively when you choose to import an existing photo from your gallery into the app to convert it into a PDF. We use the zero-permission Android Photo Picker wherever supported.
  • Internal File Storage: Used to save generated PDF documents and thumbnail previews to your device's internal storage so you can view, rename, share, or delete them.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

DocScan PDF displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob to keep the application 100% free with ₹0 server cost.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous data in accordance with Google's privacy standards, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • App performance, ad interaction data, and general crash analytics.
  • Coarse location derived from IP address (if permitted) to display relevant advertisements.

To learn more about how Google handles advertising data and how to manage your ad preferences:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

5. Sharing and User Control

When you use the "Share PDF" feature (via WhatsApp, Email, Google Drive, or Bluetooth):

  • The PDF file is shared securely through Android's standard FileProvider.
  • Only the specific recipient application you select receives access to that file.
  • You maintain full control: deleting a document from the DocScan PDF app permanently deletes both the PDF and its local thumbnail from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

DocScan PDF is a general audience utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

7. Security of Your Data

All documents and cached scan sessions are stored locally in sandbox-isolated storage provided by the Android operating system. We recommend maintaining a secure device lock (PIN, fingerprint, or password) to prevent unauthorized access to your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
